# 🎥 My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## 🧠 Overview
Stranger Things is a science fiction horror series set in the 1980s town of Hawkins, Indiana. It follows a group of kids who uncover secret experiments and a terrifying alternate dimension called the Upside Down. The show highlights friendship, courage, and the power of working together against dark forces.
